How the numbers are calculated

This calculator compares monthly plan prices over a test period, because allowances differ by plan. The default is 6 months. Rankscale Pro at $99 times 6 is $594, and Pro+ at $229 times 6 is $1,374. SE Ranking Core at $129 plus the $89 AI Search add-on is $218 a month, or $1,308 over 6 months, and SE Visible Basic at $79 is $474. Rankscale's credit model adds a second formula. Credits per refresh equal prompts times the credit rate of each engine tracked: at 0.25 credits per standard engine, 100 prompts on 3 engines is 100 times 0.75, or 75 credits, and adding Claude at 2 credits per prompt adds 200 more, for 275. Which engines count as standard, and how many credits each plan includes, should be checked on Rankscale's pricing page. The prices come from vendor pages and third-party reads in 2026 that sometimes disagree, and annual billing can lower them. We have not measured how accurately either tool detects AI Overview citations, so test both on a sample of keywords. To check this against your own bill, enter the plan prices shown on your account in Lab Settings, then compare the calculator's total with your most recent invoice. A gap of more than about 10 percent usually means a tier, billing term or add-on is missing from the estimate, so note the difference before you rely on the verdict.

FAQ

[lt_faq] What is an AI Overview tracker?|It is a tool that checks whether a search result shows an AI-generated answer for your keyword and whether your site is cited in it. Some trackers also monitor other AI assistants, so check which surfaces each plan covers. Are AI Overviews shown for every keyword?|No. They appear for some queries and not others, and this changes over time, so tracking shows where they currently appear. Focus on the keywords that matter most to revenue. Do I need a separate tool for AI Overviews?|Not necessarily. Some SEO platforms include AI tracking as an add-on, which can be cheaper if you already pay for the suite. Compare what your current tool offers before buying a second subscription. How does a credit-based plan differ from a prompt-based plan?|A prompt-based plan charges for a number of tracked prompts, while a credit-based plan charges per prompt per engine per run. Credit plans reward tracking fewer, cheaper engines and can punish expensive ones. How long should I test before committing?|Run a three to six month test on a fixed set of prompts, since AI answers vary and trends take weeks to show. Avoid annual billing until you have confirmed the data is useful. [/lt_faq]
