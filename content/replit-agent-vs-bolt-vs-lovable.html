Direct answer

  • Best for non-technical founders: Replit Core is $20 a month (older Core price $25) with $20 of usage credits, Lovable Pro is $25 with 100 credits plus 5 daily bonus credits, and Bolt Pro is $25 with 10M tokens. Under the calculator's assumptions, 100 prompts cost $30.20 on Replit, $25 on Lovable and $25 on Bolt, and 200 prompts cost $60.20, $50 and $25. Those per-prompt costs are assumptions, so measure your own on one small app first.
  • Best for builders shipping client work: Team terms differ. Lovable charges per workspace with unlimited members, Bolt Teams is $30 per member, and Replit Pro is $100 a month for up to 15 builders. Replit also bills deployments separately: static sites are free, scheduled and autoscale deployments start at $1, and reserved VMs start at $20. Check export options before you build something you depend on.
  • Pricing trap: The three tools meter differently, in dollar credits, task credits and tokens, so plan prices alone mislead. Debugging loops burn credits fast, and heavy users report $100 to $300 a month in overages on top of Replit Pro. Write a one-page spec and split the build into small prompts, since vague prompts and repeated fix attempts waste more than the first build.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 200 build prompts a month. Replit Core at $20 includes $20 of usage credits, and the calculator assumes about $0.30 per Agent checkpoint, so about 66 prompts fit and each extra costs $0.30, giving $20 plus 134 times $0.30, or $60.20. Lovable Pro at $25 includes 100 credits and the calculator assumes 1 credit per prompt with $0.25 per extra credit, an assumed rate, giving $25 plus 100 times $0.25, or $50. Bolt Pro at $25 includes 10 million tokens, and at an assumed 50,000 tokens per prompt that covers 200 prompts, so 200 prompts cost $25, with extra prompts assumed at $0.125. At 400 prompts the totals are $120.20, $100 and $50. Replit's own pricing says its Agent bills effort-priced checkpoints from about $0.06 to over $1, and Bolt's token burn grows as a project gets larger, so real costs vary widely. The plan prices were checked from July to November 2026 and these tools reprice often. We have not built the same app on all three, so we make no claim about quality or speed. Build one small app on each free plan and read the usage dashboards before you pick. To check this against your own bill, enter your account's plan prices in Lab Settings and compare the total with your latest invoice. A gap above about 10 percent usually means a missing tier or add-on.
