How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 30 posts a month, 45 manual minutes per post and $15 an hour of your time. Manual cost is posts times minutes divided by 60 times hourly value: 30 times 45 divided by 60 times $15, or $337.50. The automated workflow is a $9 Make Core plan plus about $0.03 per post of model usage. That figure is derived from published rates: a transcript of about 2,000 tokens in and a 2,000-token post out on Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 and $10 per million costs $0.004 plus $0.02, or $0.024, rounded up to $0.03. So 30 posts cost $9 plus $0.90, or $9.90. On DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at its peak rate the model cost is about $0.003 a post. Breakeven is $9 divided by the saving of $11.22 per post, under one post. At 4 posts, 5 minutes and $2 an hour, manual cost is $0.67 and automation $9.12. The model does not include editing or fact-checking, which is where most of the time goes. We have not published a batch of posts and measured traffic, so we make no claim about performance.
