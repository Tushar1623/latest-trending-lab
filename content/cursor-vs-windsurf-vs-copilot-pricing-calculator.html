  • Best for solo developers: At one seat with no extra usage, GitHub Copilot Pro is $10 a month, while Cursor Pro and Windsurf Pro are $20 (older Windsurf Pro plans were $15).   • Best for teams and agencies: Seats multiply every difference. Windsurf moved to daily and weekly quotas with overage billed at API rates. A few agent-heavy developers can double a team's bill, so track credit use per person for the first month.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 3 developer seats, each using $10 of extra credits beyond the plan allowance. The calculator charges each seat its monthly price and adds the extra usage dollar for dollar, because the tools now meter usage at or near model API rates. That gives Cursor Pro 3 times $20 plus $30, or $90, Windsurf Pro the same $90, Copilot Pro 3 times $10 plus $30, or $60, and Copilot Business 3 times $19 plus $30, or $87. At one seat with no extra usage the prices are simply $20, $20, $10 and $19. At 10 seats with $20 extra per seat they are $400, $400, $300 and $390. Pass-through pricing is a simplification: each vendor sets its own credit value and included allowance, and a tool may throttle instead of charging. We have not benchmarked completion speed or code quality here, so the calculator compares price only. Run each editor on a real repository for a week, then enter your actual extra spend from each usage dashboard to replace the assumption. To check this against your own bill, enter the plan prices shown on your account in Lab Settings, then compare the calculator's total with your most recent invoice. A gap of more than about 10 percent usually means a tier, billing term or add-on is missing from the estimate, so note the difference before you rely on the verdict.
