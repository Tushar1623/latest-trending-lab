How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 12 videos a month at 8 minutes each, or 96 finished minutes. The budget stack adds three fixed costs: Claude Pro at $20 for scripts, ElevenLabs Creator at $22 for narration and an assumed $20 for a video editing tool, for $62, plus an assumed $0.05 per finished minute of rendering credits, or $4.80, for $66.80. The premium stack swaps in ElevenLabs Pro at $99 and an assumed $60 video tool, for $179 plus $0.20 per minute, or $19.20, for $198.20. Freelancers are modelled at an assumed $6 per finished minute, or $576. Setting 62 plus 0.05 times minutes equal to 6 times minutes gives a crossover near 10.4 minutes, about one video a month. The subscription prices are published rates checked in 2026, but the video tool, rendering and freelancer figures are assumptions. We have not measured render times or audience results for this page, so treat the totals as planning estimates and replace the assumptions in Lab Settings with your own receipts. Because the largest inputs here are assumptions, time one real task from start to finish, record the minutes and the tool cost, and enter those figures.
