Direct answer

  • Best for solo builders: Start with 10 pages, not 1,000. At the calculator's assumptions of a $10 RPM and a small affiliate click-through, 50 pages at 500 visits each produce about $250 of AdSense and $180 of affiliate value against $20 of overhead, for roughly $410 a month. But 10 pages at 100 visits yield about $1.20 net, and break-even is about 23 visits per page at 50 pages. Many programmatic pages get little traffic, so test first.
  • Best for agencies: Build the data source and the page template first, then publish in batches and watch indexing and engagement before the next batch. Hosting is cheap, for example a Hostinger KVM 2 VPS at $8.99 on the introductory term and $14.99 at renewal, and generating a 2,000-token page on Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs about $0.024, so 1,000 pages cost about $24 in API fees. The cost is the unique data and review each page needs.
  • Pricing trap: Thousands of near-identical pages can be treated as low-quality scaled content, and Google's spam policies target content produced mainly to manipulate rankings. RPM varies widely by niche and country, and the master plan's $24 RPM and 8 percent affiliate click-through are optimistic against the conservative defaults here. Treat every figure as an assumption, and change it once you have your own analytics.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 50 published pages at 500 monthly visits each, or 25,000 visits. AdSense revenue is visits times RPM divided by 1,000: with an assumed $10 RPM, 25,000 visits give $250. Affiliate value assumes 2 percent of visitors click, 2 percent of those buy and each sale earns $18 in the first month, or $0.0072 per visit, giving $180. Overhead is an assumed $15 of hosting plus $0.10 per page a month, or $20. Net is $250 plus $180 minus $20, or $410. At 10 pages and 100 visits the figures are $10, $7.20 and $16, for about $1.20, and at 200 pages and 2,000 visits they are $4,000, $2,880 and $35, for about $6,845. Break-even visits per page equal overhead divided by pages times $0.0172, so about 23 at 50 pages and 93 at 10. Cost checks use published rates: a 2,000-token input and 2,000-token output page on Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs $0.024. The revenue inputs are assumptions, not forecasts, and recurring affiliate revenue would compound while the first-month value used here would not. We have not built a directory and measured traffic, so we make no claim about results. Launch 10 pages, wait for indexing, and replace each assumption with your measured figure.
