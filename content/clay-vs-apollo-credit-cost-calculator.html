How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 1,000 leads enriched at 2 credits each, or 2,000 credits. Clay Launch is $185 a month on monthly billing ($167 annual) and includes about 2,500 Data Credits, so 2,000 credits fit inside the plan and cost $185. Apollo Basic at $49 per user on annual billing includes about 2,500 credits a month, derived from 30,000 a year, so 2,000 credits cost $49. At 4,000 credits Clay needs 1,500 top-up credits at about $0.111 each, which is the plan rate of $185 divided by 2,500 credits plus the 50 percent surcharge, or $166.50 extra for $351.50, while Apollo Professional at $79 includes about 4,000 credits. Beyond Professional the calculator charges $0.02 per extra Apollo credit, an assumption at the Professional rate. A Data Credit on Clay and a credit on Apollo are not identical units, so the comparison depends on how many your workflow uses per lead. The prices were read in October 2026, and one outdated source lists Clay Launch at $60, which no longer applies. We have not measured data accuracy, email deliverability or match rates, so test both tools on a sample of 200 leads.
