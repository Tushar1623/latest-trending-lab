Direct answer

  • Best for solo builders: On published 2026 rates, Retell's cost estimator lands at about $0.115 a minute, Bland at about $0.14 and a typical Vapi stack at about $0.15, though Vapi's all-in range is $0.13 to $0.30. At 1,000 minutes a month that is $115, $140 and $150, and at 5,000 minutes the gap between Retell and Vapi grows to $175. Price your own call mix before you pick, because the cheapest platform depends on the model and voice you choose.
  • Best for agencies: Quote clients on cost per outcome, not per minute: monthly platform and phone cost divided by booked appointments or qualified leads. A bundled rate such as Bland's makes the bill predictable, while Vapi passes each provider through at cost, which gives more control and more ways to overspend. Test 50 real calls, record minutes billed and failures, and add a margin on top of the measured cost.
  • Pricing trap: Vapi advertises a $0.05 per minute platform fee, but one 2026 analysis reports first-month bills near $0.27 a minute once transcription, model, voice and phone are added. Bland states that telephony is billed separately on every tier. Always add speech recognition, the language model, text-to-speech and phone charges to the platform fee, and watch minimum billing increments on short calls.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 2,000 call minutes a month. The calculator multiplies minutes by an all-in rate per minute: $0.15 for a typical Vapi stack, $0.14 for Bland and $0.115 for Retell, giving $300, $280 and $230. Retell's own estimator breaks its $0.115 into a language model at $0.045, voice infrastructure at $0.055 and text-to-speech at $0.015, with telephony shown as zero. Vapi's $0.05 platform fee is only one layer, so the $0.15 figure assumes a mid-priced model and voice, inside its reported $0.13 to $0.30 range. Bland's $0.14 is its all-in rate reported in September 2026, although an earlier quote of $0.09 flat circulated in May, which shows how quickly these prices move. Phone numbers and carrier minutes are extra on most platforms, and concurrency limits, HIPAA add-ons and premium voices can raise the rate. We have not measured call latency or answer quality for this page. Vendors publish response-time claims, but real latency depends on the model, voice and network, so test calls before you commit. Because the largest inputs here are assumptions, time one real task from start to finish, record the minutes and the tool cost, and enter those figures. Re-run the calculator after your first month of actual use and note the date, so you can see how far the estimate drifted from reality.
