  • Best for solopreneurs: Monica Pro costs $99 a year, about $8.30 a month, and HARPA AI Individual is $12 a month on annual billing, so a two-extension stack is about $20.30 a month per seat. At $25 an hour of your time, that pays for itself after about 0.8 hours saved a month.   • Best for small teams: Agree on an approved list and check who can see the data an extension reads before anyone installs it. Monica's free plan allows 40 basic queries a day and Pro gives 5,000 basic and 200 advanced queries a month, so heavy users can hit limits. HARPA's Teams plan is about $19 a month. Prefer one or two extensions for repeated jobs over a long list.
  • Pricing trap: Extensions often request permission to read every page you visit, so a cheap or free tool can cost you privacy. Read the permissions and the privacy policy, and avoid using one on pages with client data, banking or health information. A free built-in assistant costs nothing, which makes it a fair baseline, but cost alone says nothing about value or the time you save.
