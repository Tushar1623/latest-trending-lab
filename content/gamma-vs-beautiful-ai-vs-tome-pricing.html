How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is a team of 5 on annual billing. Per-seat prices are $9 for Gamma Plus, $18 for Gamma Pro, $40 for Beautiful.ai Team and $12 for Canva Pro, which is $144 a year divided by 12. Multiplying by 5 seats gives $45, $90, $200 and $60. At 2 seats the totals are $18, $36, $80 and $24, and at 20 seats $180, $360, $800 and $240. Monthly billing costs more: Canva Pro is $18, Beautiful.ai Team $50 and Gamma Plus $10. Canva's price sources differ, with one 2026 page listing Pro at $180 a year and older ones at $120 to $144, so the calculator uses the figure shown on three independent reads from September and October 2026. Beautiful.ai's Team plan is limited to 2 through 20 users, so the seat slider stops there. Credit allowances are not converted into decks, because a credit buys different amounts depending on length and features. Tome is excluded because it shut down. Prices were checked from September to October 2026 and these vendors reprice often. We have not measured deck quality, editing speed or export fidelity, so build the same five-slide deck in each free tier, export it, and compare before you pay.
