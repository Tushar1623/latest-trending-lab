Direct answer

  • Best for solo builders: n8n Cloud Starter costs 24 euros a month billed monthly (20 euros annual) for 2,500 executions, and Pro costs 60 euros (50 annual) for 10,000. A Hostinger KVM 2 VPS costs $14.99 a month at renewal ($8.99 on the introductory term) with no execution limit. At 10,000 executions the VPS saves about $45 a month, but only while your upkeep stays under roughly 1.8 hours a month at $25 an hour.
  • Best for agencies: Business costs about 800 euros a month for 40,000 executions (sources differ), so agencies with technical staff usually self-host, and the breakeven against Business is about 31 hours of monthly upkeep. Below 2,500 executions the picture flips: Starter at 24 euros beats a VPS plus a few hours of your time, so keep small client workflows on Cloud.
  • Pricing trap: The VPS price is not the cost of self-hosting. Backups, updates, security patches and outages are your job, and Hostinger plans are billed upfront for the term and rise at renewal, for example from $8.99 to $14.99 on KVM 2. Cloud Starter also limits you to 5 concurrent executions, which long or parallel workflows can hit.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 10,000 executions a month with one hour of your time for upkeep. n8n counts executions of a whole workflow, not each step, so 10,000 runs fit the Pro plan at 60 euros a month on monthly billing. The calculator treats euros as dollars for simplicity. Self-hosting on Hostinger KVM 2 costs $14.99 at the renewal rate plus one hour at $25, or $39.99. At zero upkeep hours the VPS is $14.99, at two hours it is $64.99, and at four hours it is $114.99, so the crossover with Cloud Pro is about 1.8 hours. At 2,500 executions Starter at 24 euros against $14.99 plus time crosses at about 0.36 hours, and against Business at about 800 euros the crossover is roughly 31 hours. The $25 hourly value is an assumption you can change in Lab Settings. We have not benchmarked execution speed on either option, and a VPS that runs out of memory behaves very differently from a managed plan, so size the server for your busiest workflow. To check this against your own bill, enter the plan prices shown on your account in Lab Settings, then compare the calculator's total with your most recent invoice. A gap of more than about 10 percent usually means a tier, billing term or add-on is missing from the estimate, so note the difference before you rely on the verdict.
