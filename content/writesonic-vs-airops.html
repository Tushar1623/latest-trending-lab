How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 25 articles a month with 20 AirOps tasks per article. Writesonic's monthly prices checked from August to November 2026 are $99 for Starter with 15 AI articles and 1 user, $249 for Basic with 25 articles and 2 users, and $499 for Growth with 50 articles and 3 users. At 25 articles the calculator therefore charges $249. Above 50 articles it extends Growth at about $10 per article, which is $499 divided by 50 and not a published overage rate. AirOps is modelled as $49 plus $0.009 per task above an assumed 1,000-task allowance, so 25 articles times 20 tasks is 500 tasks and costs $49. At 50 articles and 100 articles, with 20 tasks each, it costs $49 and $58. These are formulas on list prices from third-party reads that vary between sources, so confirm both pricing pages. Editing time is excluded from both so the tool fees stay comparable. We have not run the same brief through both tools, so we make no claim about article quality or speed. Run ten briefs through each, count the minutes of editing each draft needs, and add that to the fee. To check this against your own bill, enter your account's plan prices in Lab Settings and compare the total with your latest invoice. A gap above about 10 percent usually means a missing tier or add-on.
