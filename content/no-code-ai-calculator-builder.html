How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 8 calculators with 3 revisions each. The cost is a one-off project total, shown per project, not per month. For the Claude route the calculator assumes $20 for one month of Claude Pro plus 1.5 hours per calculator and 0.25 hours per revision at an assumed $20 an hour, or $30 per calculator plus $5 per calculator per revision: 8 times $30 plus 8 times 3 times $5, plus $20, is $380. For Calconic Regular at $17 it assumes half that time, 0.75 hours per calculator and 0.15 per revision, so $15 and $3 per unit, giving $209. A freelance developer at an assumed $40 an hour with 2 hours per calculator and 0.5 per revision is $80 and $20 per unit, or $1,120. At 1 calculator and 1 revision the totals are $55, $35 and $100, and at 30 and 10 they are $2,420, $1,367 and $8,400. The Claude, Calconic and Outgrow prices were checked in 2026, but the hours and rates are assumptions, and the assumption that a hosted builder halves build time is debatable. We have not built the same calculator three ways, so we make no claim about speed or quality. Time one build each and replace the assumptions.
