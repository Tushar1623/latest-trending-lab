How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 100 articles a month at 20 tasks each, or 2,000 tasks. The calculator assumes the $49 plan includes 1,000 tasks, which the sources found do not state, and charges the remaining 1,000 at $9 per 1,000, or $0.009 per task, for $49 plus $9, or $58. At 10 articles and 20 tasks, 200 tasks, the total stays at the $49 base. At 1,000 articles and 100 tasks, 100,000 tasks, it is $49 plus 99 times $9, or $940. Freelance writers are modelled at an assumed $40 per article, so 100 articles cost $4,000, and the comparison excludes editing, fact-checking and publishing time on both sides. The AirOps figures come from 2026 third-party reviews with moderate confidence, and AirOps itself describes custom pricing for larger plans, so confirm with the vendor. Tasks per article depend on the workflow you build: a simple outline-and-draft flow may use a handful, while a research-heavy flow with several model calls and checks can use far more. We have not measured task consumption on a real workflow for this page, so run two or three articles and read your usage dashboard first. To check this against your own bill, enter the plan prices shown on your account in Lab Settings, then compare the calculator's total with your most recent invoice. A gap of more than about 10 percent usually means a tier, billing term or add-on is missing from the estimate, so note the difference before you rely on the verdict.
