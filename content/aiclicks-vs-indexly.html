How the numbers are calculated

This calculator compares monthly plan prices by team seats, because the two vendors count different things. AIclicks is modelled as flat tiers, $59 for Starter and $189 for the next tier, regardless of seats. Indexly is modelled per user, $99 for Starter and $299 for Growth, times the seats you enter. The default is 2 seats: AIclicks Starter $59, AIclicks mid tier $189, Indexly Starter $198 and Indexly Growth $598. At one seat Indexly Starter is $99 and Growth $299, and at five seats Starter is $495 and Growth $1,495. The AIclicks figures were observed in May 2026 on its pricing page and the Indexly figures were read from its pricing page in May 2026, with a 17 percent discount for annual billing reported on Indexly. The plans do different jobs, since Indexly bundles indexing and content tools while AIclicks focuses on AI visibility tracking, so this is a price comparison, not a feature ranking. We have not measured how quickly either tool gets pages indexed or how accurately it tracks citations, so test both on a small set of URLs and prompts. To check this against your own bill, enter the plan prices shown on your account in Lab Settings, then compare the calculator's total with your most recent invoice. A gap of more than about 10 percent usually means a tier, billing term or add-on is missing from the estimate, so note the difference before you rely on the verdict.
