  • Best for solo builders: At 1 hour of audio a month the calculator gives ElevenLabs Creator at $22, Cartesia Pro at $5, Fish Audio's API at $0.81 and OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini-tts at $0.90. At 10 hours it is $99 on ElevenLabs Pro, $49 on Cartesia Startup, $8.10 on Fish Audio and $9 on OpenAI. The APIs cost about a tenth as much, but they need a short script or app, while the plans include studio editors. If you can run a script, use an API.
  • Best for audiobook and podcast creators: At 30 hours ElevenLabs Scale and Cartesia Scale both cost $299, against about $24 on Fish Audio and $27 on OpenAI. Cost is not the only axis: Fish Audio offers voice cloning from a short sample, OpenAI offers 13 preset voices with no cloning, and ElevenLabs is the usual quality benchmark. Audition one chapter on each before you commit to a whole book.
  • Pricing trap: Fish Audio bills per UTF-8 byte, which equals characters for English but costs more per character for non-Latin scripts. The conversion from hours to characters is an estimate that sources put between 45,000 and 83,000 per hour, a spread of nearly 2 times. ElevenLabs overage rates differ across sources, from $0.18 to $0.30 per 1,000 characters. Play.ht was reported shut down on December 31, 2025, so ignore guides that still compare it.

How the numbers are calculated

One hour of narration is about 54,000 characters, the conversion the calculator uses. Fish Audio's pay-as-you-go API prices its S2 Pro model at $15 per million UTF-8 bytes, so an hour costs 54,000 times $15 divided by one million, or $0.81. OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini-tts costs about $0.015 per minute of audio, or $0.90 an hour. For comparison, ElevenLabs' developer API lists $50 per million characters for Flash and $100 for its v3 model, which is $2.70 and $5.40 an hour. The plans work differently. ElevenLabs Creator at $22 includes about 121,000 credits, Pro at $99 includes 600,000 and Scale at $299 includes 1.8 million, while Cartesia's Pro, Startup and Scale plans at $5, $49 and $299 include 100,000, 1.25 million and 8 million credits at one credit per character. At 10 hours, 540,000 characters, the totals are $99, $49, $8.10 and $9. If an hour is 45,000 characters, Fish Audio costs $0.675 an hour, and at 83,000 it costs $1.245. Prices were checked from July to November 2026. We have not compared voice quality, accents, latency or cloning accuracy, which is where these products differ most, so generate the same chapter in each before choosing. Prices and credit rules change often, so confirm them on each vendor's page before budgeting.
