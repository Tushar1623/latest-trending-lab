Direct answer

  • Best for solo builders: At 100 prompts across 3 engines the calculator shows Otterly Standard at $189, SE Ranking Core plus its AI Search add-on at $218 and Peec Pro at $245. At 50 prompts across 5 engines the order flips: Peec Starter at $95 plus two extra engines at $30 each comes to $155, below Otterly at about $198. Under 15 prompts, Otterly Lite at $29 is the cheapest way to start, and a spreadsheet of manually checked prompts costs nothing.
  • Best for agencies: Compare cost per prompt, then seats and engines. Otterly runs $1.89 per prompt on Standard and $1.22 on Premium ($489 for 400), while Peec runs $1.63 on Pro and $1.41 on Advanced ($495 for 350). Both list unlimited users. At 400 prompts and 3 engines the calculator gives Otterly $489, SE Ranking about $436 (extended beyond its published allowance) and Peec about $566, so volume decides the winner.
  • Pricing trap: Headline prices mislead. Profound's $99 entry plan tracks ChatGPT only, Surfer's $49 tier has no AI tracking, and SE Ranking's $89 AI add-on sits on top of a $129 base plan. Claude tracking is an Enterprise-only feature on Peec and a paid add-on on Otterly, and Peec charges $30 to $140 a month for each extra model. Check which engines your price includes before comparing totals.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 100 tracked prompts across 3 engines. Otterly's published ladder is $29 for 15 prompts, $189 for 100 and $489 for 400, with 100 extra prompts at $99, so 100 prompts fall on Standard at $189 and three engines add nothing because the calculator assumes four are included. Peec's ladder is $95 for 50 prompts, $245 for 150 and $495 for 350, with three engines included, so 100 prompts need Pro at $245. SE Ranking is modelled as the $129 Core plan plus the $89 AI Search add-on, or $218, assumed to cover 200 prompts and five engines, although SE Ranking does not publish the prompt allowance. At 50 prompts and 5 engines, Otterly is $189 plus $9 for one engine above four, or $198, Peec is $95 plus two engines at $30, or $155, and SE Ranking stays $218. Beyond each top tier the calculator extends at the published add-on or top-tier cost per prompt. These are formulas on list prices checked in October and November 2026, billed monthly. AI answers change between runs, so prompt counts and refresh frequency matter as much as price. We have not measured tracker accuracy. To check this against your own bill, enter your account's plan prices in Lab Settings and compare the total with your latest invoice. A gap above about 10 percent usually means a missing tier or add-on.

FAQ

[lt_faq] What is generative engine optimization?|It is the practice of improving how often and how accurately your brand appears in answers from AI assistants and AI search features. Trackers measure that visibility by running a fixed list of prompts on a schedule and recording whether you are mentioned or cited. Can I track AI visibility without paying for a tool?|Yes. Write 20 to 50 buyer questions, run them on the engines your customers use every week, and record whether your brand appears and which sources are cited. It takes time, but it costs nothing and gives you a baseline before you buy. How reliable are AI visibility numbers?|They are samples. AI answers vary between runs, users and locations, so a single day's score can mislead. Compare trends over several weeks and use the same prompts each time. How many prompts and engines do I need?|Start with the questions that matter most to revenue, usually 25 to 100, and the two or three engines your audience uses most. Each extra engine can raise the price noticeably, so add engines only when the data would change a decision. Can I switch tools later?|Usually, but check whether you can export your prompt history first. Without an export you lose your baseline, and a new tool may count prompts or engines differently.
