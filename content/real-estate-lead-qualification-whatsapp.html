How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 200 new leads a month, 8 manual minutes per lead and $8 an hour of staff time, all inputs you can change. Manual cost is leads times minutes divided by 60 times hourly cost, which is 200 times 8 divided by 60 times $8, or $213.33. The automated workflow is a $9 Make Core plan plus $0.045 per lead. That per-lead figure combines Meta's list rates for India with model usage. One marketing template at 0.8631 rupees plus five replies at about 0.115 rupees is 1.4381 rupees, about $0.018 at the roughly 80.7 rupees per dollar implied by Meta's rate card. A six-message conversation of about 1,500 input and 150 output tokens per message on Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 and $10 per million costs about $0.027. Together that is about $0.045, so 200 leads cost $9 plus $9, or $18. Breakeven is $9 divided by the saving of $1.02 per lead, about 9 leads. Meta's first 1,000 service messages per number each month are free, so volume under about 200 leads can cost less. Provider fees and GST are not included. We have not measured reply speed, lead quality or conversion. Run the workflow on 50 real leads and replace the assumptions with your own figures.
