How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 1,000 runs a month of a 3-step workflow. Make bills credits per module run, so the workload is 1,000 times 3, or 3,000 credits, which fits inside Make Core's 10,000 at $9 a month on annual billing. Zapier bills tasks per action step. If every step counts, that is also 3,000 tasks. The published entry tiers are $19.99 for 750 tasks and $49 for 2,000 on annual billing, so 3,000 tasks falls above both, and the calculator approximates the excess at $0.01 per extra task, giving $59. If Zapier does not count the trigger, 1,000 runs of two actions is 2,000 tasks, which fits the $49 tier. At 5,000 runs of 4 steps, 20,000 units, Make's approximated ladder lands at about $16 and Zapier at 49 plus 18,000 times $0.01, or $229. The ladders above the published entry tiers differ between sources, so treat the totals as estimates. We have not measured execution speed or polling intervals, and overage behavior differs by plan, so read the plan terms before relying on a tier. To check this against your own bill, enter the plan prices shown on your account in Lab Settings, then compare the calculator's total with your most recent invoice. A gap of more than about 10 percent usually means a tier, billing term or add-on is missing from the estimate, so note the difference before you rely on the verdict.
