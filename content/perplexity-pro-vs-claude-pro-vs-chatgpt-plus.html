Direct answer

  • Best for solo researchers: ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro and Perplexity Pro are all $20 a month, so the decision is about fit, not price. Perplexity is built around live web search with citations, while general assistants suit long documents, analysis and writing. Run the same five research tasks on each free tier or one-month trial, and check which one you open first the next morning.
  • Best for teams: ChatGPT Business and Claude Team standard seats are $25 a month billed monthly ($20 annual) with a two-seat minimum, and Perplexity Enterprise Pro is about $40 per seat. At 5 seats with 40 searches a day each, the calculator gives $125, $125 and $200 against about $70 for a pay-as-you-go search API stack. The API stack wins up to about 71 searches a day per person against a $25 seat and about 114 against a $40 seat.
  • Pricing trap: Seat prices look alike, but usage limits differ. Claude Team uses a rolling five-hour window plus weekly limits, and premium seats cost $100 to $125 a month. A pay-as-you-go stack has no cap, so a 25-seat team at 200 searches a day each reaches about $1,760 a month. Always compare limits, features and data controls, not only the seat price.

How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 5 seats with 40 searches per person per day over 22 working days, or 4,400 searches a month. Subscription costs are seats times price: ChatGPT Business and Claude Team at $25 give $125 and Perplexity Enterprise Pro at $40 gives $200. The pay-as-you-go option combines Perplexity's Search API at $5 per 1,000 requests, or $0.005 a search, with Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 and $10 per million tokens to write each answer, assuming 3,000 input tokens and 500 output tokens, or $0.011. That is $0.016 per search, so 4,400 searches cost about $70.40. At 2 seats and 10 searches a day the totals are $50, $50, $80 and about $7, and at 25 seats and 200 searches a day they are $625, $625, $1,000 and $1,760. Breakeven against a $25 seat is $25 divided by $0.016 times 22 days, or about 71 searches a day. The API option has no interface, no apps and no support, which the price comparison cannot capture. We have not compared answer accuracy, citation quality or speed, and those matter more than a few dollars a seat. Test all three on your own questions. To check this against your own bill, enter your account's plan prices in Lab Settings and compare the total with your latest invoice. A gap above about 10 percent usually means a missing tier or add-on.
