How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 100 tracked prompts. Otterly's ladder, $29 for 15 prompts, $189 for 100 and $489 for 400, puts 100 prompts on Standard at $189. Peec's ladder, $95 for 50, $245 for 150 and $495 for 350, puts 100 prompts on Pro at $245, so Otterly is $56 cheaper at that volume. Cost per prompt at full use is $1.93, $1.89 and $1.22 for Otterly's three tiers and $1.90, $1.63 and $1.41 for Peec's. Crossovers follow from the ladders: at 50 prompts Peec costs $95 against Otterly's $189, at 150 prompts Peec costs $245 against Otterly's $489, and at 200 prompts Otterly's $489 undercuts Peec's $495. Beyond the top published tier the calculator charges $0.99 per extra prompt for Otterly, its published add-on, and $1.41 for Peec, its top-tier rate. Profound is not modelled because its entry price covers one engine. These are formulas on monthly list prices checked in October and November 2026. We have not measured refresh speed, citation accuracy or support quality, so use free trials to compare them on your own prompts.
