How the numbers are calculated

The default scenario is 300 tickets a month that need a human and 500 that an AI agent could resolve, with an assumed $4 per human-handled ticket. Human-only support handles all 800 tickets: 800 times $4, or $3,200. With the agent, humans still handle 300 tickets at $4, or $1,200, and the agent resolves 500 at Intercom's published $0.99 per resolution, or $495, for $1,695. The published terms include a minimum of 50 resolutions a month, so the first 50 cost $49.50 even if fewer are resolved. At 50 resolvable tickets the totals are $1,400 against $1,249.50, and with none the agent plan still costs $49.50, so human-only is $1,200 against $1,249.50. The payback point is where 4 times resolvable tickets exceeds $49.50, about 13 tickets. A do-it-yourself agent costs far less in tokens, about 8,000 input and 600 output tokens per conversation on Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 and $10 per million, or about $0.022, before build and upkeep. The Fin price comes from a 2026 review, and the $4 figure is an assumption. We have not measured resolution rates or customer satisfaction, and these vary with your catalog.
