Direct answer

  • Best for solo builders: Fix the basics before anything else: let AI crawlers reach your pages in robots.txt, answer one question in the first two sentences of each page, keep accurate structured data and show a last-verified date. The llms.txt generator above is free, but major AI search providers have not confirmed that they use llms.txt, so treat it as a low-cost experiment rather than a ranking lever.
  • Best for agencies: Prove changes with a fixed prompt set. Track 15 prompts on Otterly Lite at $29 a month ($1.93 per prompt) or 50 prompts on Peec Starter at $95 ($1.90 each) for four weeks before changes and four weeks after. Rewriting 100 pages with Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs about $2.40 in API fees at 2,000 tokens in and out per page, so the real cost is the editing and review time.
  • Pricing trap: No one can guarantee citations in AI answers, and any service that promises them is selling hope. AI answers vary between runs, so a single before-and-after screenshot proves little. Blocking AI crawlers by accident in robots.txt, or hiding key facts in images and scripts, can cost you more visibility than any markup adds.
