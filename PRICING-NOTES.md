# Pricing notes (checked November 2026)

These are the figures behind the calculators and tables, with the conflicts I found. They come from vendor pages and third-party price trackers read through web search on 15 and 17 November 2026. I could not open every vendor's own pricing page, so confirm each figure on the vendor's site before you publish, then update the date in Lab Settings.

## Round 2 (17 November 2026): gaps closed
- Tome: its presentation product shut down on 30 April 2025 (many sources). It is replaced by Canva in the presentation comparison.
- Play.ht: reported shut down on 31 December 2025. It is replaced by Fish Audio and OpenAI text-to-speech.
- Pinecone and Supabase: Pinecone Standard has a $50 monthly minimum plus usage; Supabase Pro is $25. Read units per query and bytes per chunk are assumptions, flagged in the calculator note.
- Midjourney and Magnific: Midjourney Standard $30, Magnific Premium $20; the traditional studio rate uses ArchiCGI's published $300 starting price for a residential exterior still.
- Scalenut and Frase: Scalenut's current plans are Starter $59, Plus $89 and Professional $199 (older names are deprecated). Frase is Solo $15, Basic $45 and Team $115.
- Argil: Classic $39, Pro $149 and Scale $499, from Argil's own plan documentation.
- WhatsApp Business: Meta's India list rates are about 0.8631 rupees for marketing and about 0.115 for utility; service replies are charged at the utility rate from 1 October 2026 after 1,000 free per number a month.

## Conflicts that remain (confirm on the vendor's page)
- Canva Pro: $144 a year in most reads, $180 in one, $120 to $144 in older ones.
- Scalenut and Frase article allowances: from third-party reviews, not vendor pages.
- Synthesia and HeyGen minute allowances differ between sources; the calculators use the newest.
- ElevenLabs overage ($0.18 to $0.30 per 1,000 characters) and characters per audio hour (45,000 to 83,000).
- Pinecone read units per query depend on index size; Supabase compute needs grow with index size.
- DeepSeek, Make and Zapier higher tiers, n8n Business, Rankscale Pro tiers and SE Ranking's AI add-on differ between sources.
- Assumptions you set yourself: staff time, hourly rates, the freelance developer rate, RPM and conversion rates.
