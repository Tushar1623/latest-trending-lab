What Latest & Trending is

Latest & Trending is an independent lab that publishes live AI pricing calculators, tool-versus-tool cost comparisons and no-code workflow blueprints. Our goal is simple: show what AI tools and automations really cost before you pay for them.

What you will find here

  • Calculators that compare API costs, subscriptions, automation platforms and AI tool stacks at your own usage level.
  • Comparisons that explain credit systems, overage rules and other pricing traps.
  • Copy-paste workflow prompts you can use in your own work.

How we fund the site

The site earns money from display advertising and from affiliate links. Commissions never change the numbers we publish. Read our methodology and disclaimer for details.
