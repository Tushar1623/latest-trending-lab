How we calculate

Every calculator on {{site}} is a formula. We take a vendor's published prices, write down how that vendor meters usage (tokens, credits, tasks, seats or minutes), and turn it into a monthly cost for the volume you enter. The date at the top of each page is the last date we re-read the vendors' pricing pages.

Sources

We use the vendor's own pricing page and documentation first. Where a figure could not be confirmed there, we say so on the page and in the calculator note. Some tiers are approximations, and each page states which ones.

Worked examples, not lab tests

The sections called "How the numbers are calculated" are worked examples of the formulas. They are arithmetic on published rates and are not measurements of speed, quality or real-world billing. When we publish a measured result, such as an actual invoice, a usage dashboard screenshot or a timed run, we label it as measured and show the date and settings.

Assumptions you can change

Staff time, hourly rates, prompt sizes and conversion rates are assumptions. They are marked as assumptions, and you can replace them with your own numbers. Results are estimates for planning, not quotes.

Independence and corrections

Affiliate relationships do not change our figures. If you find an error, email {{email}} and we will review it and correct the page.
